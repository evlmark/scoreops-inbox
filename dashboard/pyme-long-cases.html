Methodology

Cases were identified using Jira's own "Task ID reopen" and "Task ID escalation" custom fields on IT project tickets created or active during the week of Sep 14–20, 2026. This surfaced 9 candidate tickets; one (IT-528788) was excluded because it was created a day after the week's window and resolved within 24 hours despite the escalation flag.

Revision note: the first version of this page pulled chats only from a Sep 14–20-scoped export, which — for cases that are "long" precisely because they run past the ticket's nominal week — silently cut off the story before it actually ended. This version instead pulls each customer's complete conversation history via the dashboard's own /customers/{id} endpoint (the same data source the Users tab uses), so every chat and call tied to the customer's ID is included, not just the ones whose timestamp fell inside one calendar week. Clearly unrelated side-topics (e.g. an Uber billing dispute mixed into one customer's history) are excluded and marked as such rather than silently dropped.

Finding 1 — "Resolved" ≠ actually fixed: for 4 of the 8 cases (IT-522714, IT-523204, IT-526437, IT-527423), the support ticket was marked resolved/closed in Jira, but the customer's own chat history continues past that resolution date with the same unresolved problem — in two cases (IT-523204, IT-527423) all the way to the most recent data available (Sep 25 and a new ticket due Oct 7, respectively). The Jira "resolved" timestamp reflects a front-line agent closing their part of the interaction, not confirmation that the customer's underlying issue — the account, the address, the account number — was actually delivered.
Finding 2 — recurring product bug: in at least 2 of 8 cases (IT-523204, IT-527563), the customer explicitly applied to open a business account as a legal entity (Persona Moral) and the system instead created it as an individual with business activity (PFAE) — a wrong-account-type bug, not a review-speed issue. Both customers had to notice this themselves (wrong name/legal form showing) and get it manually corrected.
Finding 3 — no calls, but one call-type record: despite explicit re-checking, only one of the 8 customers has a conversation flagged type: call in the system (IT-527563, customer Jesús, 31 Aug). Its "transcript" turned out to be chat messages an agent sent after a phone call ("Bienvenido al chat de soporte. Por este medio me puedes compartir la información que te solicité por llamada.") — there is no actual voice transcript captured anywhere in the platform for any of these 8 customers. Everything below is chat.

Разбор с продуктом: где продукт не закрывает потребность клиента

Из 9 тикетов недели (8 выше + IT-528788) отобраны случаи, в которых клиент получил бы результат только при изменении продукта — ни агент, ни специализированная команда не могли дать ему нужное в рамках текущего функционала. Сгруппировано по типу пробела; у каждого — что клиент хотел сделать, что продукт делает сейчас, цитата клиента (RU-перевод), последствие и вопрос к продукту. Скорость саппорта и качество ответов агентов здесь не оцениваются — это в карточках кейсов ниже.
