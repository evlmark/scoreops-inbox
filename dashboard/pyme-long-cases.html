Methodology

Cases were identified using Jira's own "Task ID reopen" and "Task ID escalation" custom fields on IT project tickets created or active during the week of Sep 14–20, 2026 — i.e. tickets the support team itself formally reopened or escalated, not a text-search heuristic over chat content. This surfaced 9 candidate tickets; one (IT-528788) was excluded from the deep-dive below because it was created a day after the week's window and was fully resolved within 24 hours despite the escalation flag, so it doesn't represent a genuinely long-running case.

For each of the remaining 8 cases, every chat conversation tied to the customer's ID was pulled (client + agent turns, not just the client side) and translated from Spanish to Russian below. No call transcripts exist for any of these 8 customers this week — all contact was via chat.

Pattern: 5 of the 8 cases (IT-522714, IT-523204, IT-526009, IT-527184, IT-527563) are all Corporate Banking / Lending — business account origination tickets where the customer is stuck waiting on an internal KYB/AML review or document-verification step with no proactive update, and is forced to chase support repeatedly to get even a "still waiting" answer. In three of those five, the agent's own reply to a re-opened ticket was the same generic template ("the review is taking longer than expected / please use this link to continue"), suggesting the front-line agent has no real visibility into *why* the origination review is stuck — only the specialist team does, and it doesn't proactively notify either the agent or the customer.
